## Project 8 and neutrino mass

- [Project 8 Collaboration](https://www.project8.org/) — Neutrino mass, CRES, and collaboration updates.
- [Project 8 · Experiment](https://www.project8.org/about) — The tritium endpoint, CRES, and the atomic-tritium program.
- [Pettus Lab · Indiana University](https://www.physics.indiana.edu/about/directory/all-faculty-scientists/pettus-walter.html) — Neutrino mass and double-beta decay research at Indiana University.
- [Project 8 GitHub](https://github.com/project8) — The collaboration’s software organization.
- [CRES Tritium Beta Spectrum and Neutrino Mass Limit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.05048) — Project 8’s first frequency-based neutrino mass limit.
- [CRES Phase II Apparatus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12055) — The Phase II apparatus for tritium and krypton-83m electron measurements.
- [Project 8 Mainz](https://project8.physik.uni-mainz.de/) — Public research on the atomic beam source.
- [KATRIN](https://www.katrin.kit.edu/) — A comparison point for direct neutrino-mass measurements.
- [Yacora Documentation](https://www.yacora.de/help/help-page) — Collisional-radiative modeling documentation.

## Positronium and QED

- [PSI 2025 · Testing QED and Beyond with ortho-Positronium](https://indico.psi.ch/event/16492/contributions/58062/) — Beining Rao’s PSI contribution on positronium spectra, simulation, and experiment.
- [musrSim-pos](https://github.com/BeiningRao/musrSim-pos) — Personal public positronium simulation repository.
- [Geant4 · Ortho-Positronium](https://geant4.web.cern.ch/documentation/dev/prm_html/PhysicsReferenceManual/decay/OrthoPositronium.html) — The Geant4 reference for ortho-positronium decay.
- [Geant4 · Compton Scattering](https://geant4.web.cern.ch/documentation/dev/prm_html/PhysicsReferenceManual/electromagnetic/gamma_incident/compton/compton.html) — Compton scattering models for spectrum analysis.
- [First-order correction to the o-Ps three-photon spectrum](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0506213) — Adkins’s paper on the first-order correction to the three-photon decay spectrum.
